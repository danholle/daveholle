The Life of Dave Holle

This is a compact, chronological collection of snippets about Dave's life, from his birth in 1953, to his recent passing in 2025.

It is inherently a work in progress... morphing as the content evolves, growing in breadth and depth until Dave's Celebration of Life gathering expected to be sometime around April-May of 2026.

If you would like to contribute to this timeline, just send an email to dave@holle.family, and I'll add your story. Feel free to optionally add details like

  • date (approximate is fine),
  • photos if you have them, and
  • what name you'd like in the attribution line, if any (e.g. anonymous, Phineas, Phineas Q Tumbleweed III, or...)

October 22, 1953

Dave's Best Day Ever (at that point)

David Henry Holle was born this day.

  • The name "David" came from David Seever, Dave's great uncle, who was born Swiss but supported the US WWII war effort as a fluent French speaker / interpreter. Our father Donald violently disagreed with Uncle David's politics, but their conversations moved Dad to name his first born son after him.
  • His middle name, Henry, comes from Dave's maternal grandfather, Henry Martin Woaler, who came across the Atlantic from a small town east of Oslo, Norway. He settled first in Canada, then moved south to the warm, southern environs of Illinois.

October 26, 1961

Join the Army!

Dave made the rather dramatic decision to change careers, dumping the corporate IT gig and pursuing a career in his first love: drawing.

It may be that he got the idea from Dad. They have some common ground here...

October 26, 1961

Fort Knox

For 2 years, methinks.

Worked as illustrator. His scope to excel was pretty limited. He did the job, but had little chance to be a star performer.

But we stuck together as a family, even though Dave was 400 miles away.

(story of the 2000 mile weekend, and the cartoon drawing in Alabama on Easter weekend)

1978-1982?

Terre Haute

After Fort Knox, Dave rolled up his sleeves and cracked on with an effort to become a professional cartoonist. This is no small feat: ask Scott Adams, who (before becoming the most successful cartoonist of all time) suffered through a period where no sane person would continue.

Dave went through the same gauntlet, and for him, it just didn't work.

Did he break into the big leagues? Yes. But getting $300 for a top-notch piece of work, from the likes of the Cosmopolitan or the Atlantic, just is not a sustainable thing. ...

1982-2005

Interlude

Dave went through a rough patch... not sure how to present it, because I really wasn't there. But I think he went through a number of phases:

  • New England. A period of time in Cape Cod, and in New Hampshire.
  • Triton. He lived with Mom for a while.
  • Dad. He lived with Dad for a long time, at 815, and at the apartment out by Portillo's
This period is a bit of a muddle for me... any structure to it that I don't know about? Maybe it's an extended period of lost time. If so, I can find some way of presenting it. But it would be easier if I had more of a handle on this period than I do.
